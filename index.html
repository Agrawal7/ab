Dear Anushka,

I thought about just sending a text, but “happy birthday” felt a little too small for eighteen years of you. So this is me making an effort. Don’t get used to it.

Your politeness isn’t just a nice habit — it’s the way you make people feel considered, even when you don’t have to. And you’re funnier than you give yourself credit for. You’ll be quiet for a bit, then say one perfectly timed thing and the whole room is gone.

When you’re comfortable, you talk with your hands and your whole face, and the stories come with three side plots. Those are my favourite conversations, honestly. Not the loud ones — the easy ones.

Our friendship is the easy kind too. No performances, no keeping score. We can go quiet for a while, pick right back up, and it still feels the same. I don’t take that for granted.

So here’s to eighteen: more easy days, more ridiculous stories, and more rooms where you feel comfortable enough to be the loudest one in them.

Happy birthday, Anushka. Glad you’re here.

Cheers,
your friend

P.S. You’re legally allowed to be a little dramatic today. I checked.
